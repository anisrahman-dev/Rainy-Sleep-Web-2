Strong Rain & Thunderstorm on an Old House — 10 Hours of Storm Sounds for Sleep

Ten unbroken hours of strong rain and intense thunder, recorded as if you were tucked inside an old house at night. A full overnight storm — heavier, denser, and more dramatic than a gentle rainfall — for the deepest possible sleep.

This isn't a soft, polite rainfall. It's heavy rain and thunder at full intensity — a dense, relentless downpour with thunder clapping close enough to feel, captured by LUCASTA from inside an old house at night. The kind of storm you'd normally rush home to get out of, recorded so you can sit through ten hours of it from the safest place imaginable: bed.

The "old house" framing is the magic of this one. There's no specific cabin, no alpine chalet, no architectural style to picture — just a creaky, weathered home that could be any house you've ever lived in. That familiarity is what lets the storm outside feel cinematic without ever feeling foreign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is "strong" thunder too loud for sleep?expand_more

Only if you play it at full volume. Strong storm recordings have a wider dynamic range than gentle rain, so set your device to roughly half of where you'd normally have music. The rain layer becomes a steady blanket, and the thunder rolls underneath it without ever startling you. If a single clap feels too sharp, drop the volume one notch and you're set for the whole night.

Does YouTube interrupt this with ads at night?expand_more

Yes — long videos like this one almost always carry mid-roll ads, and they often play at random points through the night. There's no reliable way to disable them from the YouTube player itself unless you have Premium. If even one woken-up-by-an-ad moment is too many, switch to the ad-free Rainy Mood player and you'll never hit a commercial.

Can I leave this playing for back-to-back nights?expand_more

Absolutely. Ten hours is more than one night of sleep, so most people just press play before bed and stop it in the morning. If you want it on permanent loop across multiple nights, right-click the player and choose "Loop" — YouTube will restart it as soon as it ends. For seamless multi-night sessions without any gap or ad break between loops, Rainy Mood handles that natively in the background.
