Frequently Asked Questions

Is one hour of rain sounds enough to fall asleep?expand_more

For most listeners, yes — sleep onset usually arrives inside 10–20 minutes. If you wake easily, loop the video or use Rainy Mood for continuous all-night playback.

Will the thunderclaps wake me up?expand_more

No — the thunder is mostly distant and rolling, woven into the rain. Keep the volume moderate and the brain quickly learns to read the rumble as part of the safe backdrop.

Is windy rain better than steady rain for sleep?expand_more

Plenty of listeners prefer windy rain because the gusts add a slow, breath-like rhythm. Steady rain is more uniform; both work — try this one and see which your brain settles into.

Can I play this while working or studying?expand_more

Yes. A powerful thunderstorm is wonderful focus background — no lyrics, no surprises, just immersive ambient density that smothers office chatter and notifications.
