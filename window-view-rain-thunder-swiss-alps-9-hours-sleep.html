Window View with Rain & Thunder — Nine Hours of Swiss Alps Rain Sounds for Sleep

An unbroken nine-hour window onto Wengen in the Swiss Alps — clouds drifting over the high peaks, valley cottages dimming under the rain, a slow storm passing the glass. Eyes have a place to rest. Ears have a downpour.

Rainy Mood•May 25, 2026•6 min read

There is a particular kind of calm that only arrives when you are inside, dry, and watching weather move past a window. This nine-hour piece from Relaxing Ambience ASMR captures it almost too well — rain and thunder over the village of Wengen in the Swiss Alps, with clouds spilling slowly over the ridgelines while cottages in the valley fade in and out of the downpour.

Unlike a fixed-camera roof loop or a tight ASMR window shot, this one gives the eyes somewhere to wander. The horizon line is steady, the weather is not, and the soundtrack is real water hitting real glass. Open it in a tab during work, leave it on a TV in the evening, or let it run through the night.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I watch the video or just listen?expand_more

Either works, and the right choice depends on the moment. For evening wind-downs, leave the picture on — the cloud movement is part of what slows the eyes. For overnight sleep, dim the screen or close your eyes and let the rain do the rest. For focused work, push the video to a second monitor at low brightness.

Does the visual movement disturb sleep?expand_more

Generally not — the cloud and rain movement here is slow, low-contrast, and predictable, which is the opposite of what alerts the brain. The real sleep risk is screen brightness, not motion. Drop the display to its lowest setting, point it away from your face, or switch to a black-screen audio player like Rainy Mood once you are settled.

Where in Switzerland was this filmed?expand_more

Wengen — a car-free Alpine village in the Bernese Oberland, perched on a ledge above the Lauterbrunnen valley. The view looks out over scattered cottages and the peaks beyond, which is why the clouds appear to slide horizontally past the window rather than overhead.
