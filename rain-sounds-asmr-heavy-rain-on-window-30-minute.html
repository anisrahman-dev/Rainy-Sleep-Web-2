Frequently Asked Questions

Do rain sounds really help ADHD?expand_more

Yes. Steady ambient noise like heavy rain on a window gives an ADHD brain a baseline of predictable stimulation, which frees focus. Many ADHD listeners report that rain ASMR helps both sleep and concentration.

Is 30 minutes long enough to fall asleep?expand_more

For most listeners, yes. Average sleep onset on a calm ASMR track sits around 10–20 minutes. If you wake easily, loop the video or pick a one-hour-plus version.

What makes rain on a window "ASMR"?expand_more

The high-frequency taps of rain on glass trigger the same tingly relaxation response many people feel from whispering or tapping — but without a human voice, which makes it safer for sleep.
