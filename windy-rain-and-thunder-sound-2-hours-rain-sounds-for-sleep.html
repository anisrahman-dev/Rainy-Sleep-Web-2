Frequently Asked Questions

Is the sound of wind calming or unsettling for sleep?expand_more

For most people, wind paired with rain feels deeply calming — it brings a breath-like rhythm. Strong wind on its own (without rain) can feel uneasy; the rain anchors it.

Will the thunder wake my baby?expand_more

Played quietly, the thunder folds into the rain. For very young babies, choose pure rain tracks without thunder (see our forest rain post).

How does this compare to a hurricane sound?expand_more

It's hurricane-adjacent — heavy windy rain and thunder, just without the extreme high-wind peaks. Dramatic, but still safe for sleep.
