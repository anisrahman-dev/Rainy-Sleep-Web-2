When You'll Hear Back

We are a small crew, so expect a reply within roughly 2 to 5 business days. Privacy and legal requests are handled within the 30-day window that GDPR and CCPA require.

Reporting a Bug

If you are writing about a bug, it helps to include:

  • Your device and operating system (for example iPhone 15 on iOS 18, Pixel 8 on Android 14, or MacBook running Chrome 124).
  • What you were trying to do at the time.
  • What actually happened instead.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording if you can grab one.
