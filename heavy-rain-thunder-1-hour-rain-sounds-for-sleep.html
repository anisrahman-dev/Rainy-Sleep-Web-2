Frequently Asked Questions

Heavy rain or gentle rain — which is better for sleep?expand_more

Heavy rain blankets more outside noise, so it usually wins in louder environments. Gentle rain is softer and better suited to ASMR and meditation. Try each at your normal sleep volume.

Does thunder disturb sleep?expand_more

At a moderate volume, no — the thunder is mostly distant rumble. Avoid pushing the volume; the brain hears sudden, loud thunderclaps as alarm cues.

How do I loop this to play all night?expand_more
