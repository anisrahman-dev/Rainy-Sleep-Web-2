Our Story

About Rainy Mood

Clean rain sounds for sleep, focus, and meditation. No ads, no accounts, no algorithms. Just the storm itself.

Why Rainy Mood Exists

Most rain players and YouTube uploads break the one thing you came for: quiet. Mid-roll ads startle you awake. Surprise autoplay yanks you out of focus. Audible loop seams give the brain a pattern to chase. Rainy Mood was made to fix all of that.

Our rain is synthesized live in your browser through the Web Audio API. Every droplet, gust, and rumble of thunder is generated in the moment, so nothing repeats and your ears never settle into a pattern. The result is rain that feels real, runs as long as you need, and gets out of the way.

  • Travelers covering up unfamiliar hotel and airport noise on the road.

How It's Made

Rainy Mood lives entirely inside your browser. The audio is built on the client with the Web Audio API, so there is no streaming server, no media buffer, and almost no bandwidth used. That is why it keeps playing offline once loaded, never repeats, and sips a tiny fraction of the data a ten-hour video would.

The rain you see on the canvas is drawn live as well. Each droplet is shaped by the same physics engine that sculpts the sound.

Rainy Mood is the work of a small, independent group of designers, developers, and audio engineers who care about rest, focus, and the quiet pleasure of listening to rain tap a window. There are no investors, no shareholders, and no grow-at-all-costs plan. Just the rain.
