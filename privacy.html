Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Rainy Mood ("we", "us", "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect when you use our website and mobile app, how that information is used, and the choices available to you. By using Rainy Mood, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Gather

We aim to collect the minimum amount of personal data necessary. The categories of data may include:

  • Automatically collected data. Device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate region (derived from your IP address), crash logs, and anonymous usage analytics (for instance, which presets are played most often).
  • Information you provide. When you reach out by email, we receive your email address along with the contents of your message.
  • Cookies & local storage. Our site uses local storage to remember your volume, mixer, and theme preferences. We do not employ cross-site tracking cookies.

We do not collect: precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, calendar, SMS, or financial information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run and improve the website and app.
  • To resolve bugs and investigate crashes.
  • To learn which features people find most valuable.
  • To reply to support requests that you send to us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share a limited amount of data with:

  • Service providers who support our operations (such as analytics, crash reporting, and hosting). They are contractually obligated to use data solely for our purposes.
  • Legal authorities where required by law, by court order, or to safeguard our rights.
  • Successors in the case of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

4. Children's Privacy

Rainy Mood is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

Usage and crash logs are retained for up to 24 months. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to address your request, after which they are archived or deleted. You may ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, as well as to object to or opt out of certain types of processing. To exercise any of these rights, please email us at the address below.

Users in the EU/UK have rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, which include the right to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

7. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, access controls, and encrypted storage where applicable). However, no internet transmission method is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may include links to YouTube, Google Play, and other external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own respective policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use Rainy Mood after such updates means you accept them.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, write to hello@rainymood.com, or head over to our contact page.
