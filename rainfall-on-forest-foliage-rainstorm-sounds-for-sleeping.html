Frequently Asked Questions

Why does rain on leaves feel so calming?expand_more

Leaves scatter each drop, softening the sharp attack and leaving a downy, breath-like texture the brain reads as completely safe.

Is rain without thunder better for babies?expand_more

Often, yes — a sudden thunderclap can pop an infant out of light sleep. Pure rain provides continuous masking without those peaks.

How does this compare to rain on a window?expand_more

Window rain has sharper tapping highs (more ASMR). Forest rain is softer and more diffused (more meditative). Both are excellent — try them both.
